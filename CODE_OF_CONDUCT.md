# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Crestwright to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses, tests, or
contributes to it. We pledge to make participation free of harassment and
discrimination, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good faith and criticise ideas, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Respect the licence and the rights of third parties: do not post
  proprietary presets, third-party measurement data, or code you cannot
  licence under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, close issues, or block contributors whose behaviour violates this
code.

This code applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other channel used for the project.
